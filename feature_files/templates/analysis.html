About This Analysis

SD Touch Tracking: Records when the spread touches various standard deviation levels and tracks whether it subsequently returns to the mean.

Success Rate: The percentage of times the spread returned to mean after touching that SD level.

Limit Order Stats: Performance metrics for pegged limit order execution.

Data is collected while the engine is running.
